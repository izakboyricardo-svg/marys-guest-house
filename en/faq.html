Frequently asked questions

How do I request a reservation?

Choose your dates and a room. The request form works locally and does not confirm a reservation.

Is availability checked in real time?

No. The website is not connected to a booking system. Availability must be confirmed by the property.

Which currency are rates charged in?

Prices are set and charged in XOF. Other currencies are indicative estimates.

How can I contact the property?

Call +228 93 54 15 12 or use the Contact page.
