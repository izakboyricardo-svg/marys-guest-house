ABOUT US

A welcoming place to stay and unwind

Mary’s Guest House welcomes you to Hédzranawoé, Lomé, in a pleasant setting that brings together accommodation, dining and conference room rental. Whether you are looking for a comfortable stay, a place to share a meal, or a space for a meeting, training session, conference or private event, our venue offers spaces suited to your needs. Explore our rooms, restaurant and conference room, and contact us for more information.
