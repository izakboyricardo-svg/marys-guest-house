Bookings

When you book, your name, contact details, stay dates, guest count and any information you choose to provide are sent to Supabase to check availability and record the reservation. Reservation records are accessible only to the authorized owner. The website does not collect online payments; payment is made directly at the property.

For requests about your data or a cancellation, contact the property using the details below.
